Healthy Weight Goals

Weight and blood sugar are closely connected. GLPro is designed to complement a healthy eating and activity plan, not replace one.

Why it matters

The CDC notes that modest weight loss and regular activity significantly lower the risk of type 2 diabetes in people with prediabetes.

How GLPro fits

By supporting steadier glucose and fewer cravings, GLPro aims to make healthy choices easier to stick with.

Habits that help

Aim for regular activity, high-fibre meals and realistic, gradual goals agreed with your healthcare provider.

Related ingredients

A Southeast Asian leaf that naturally contains corosolic acid, a compound researched in relation to glucose handling.
