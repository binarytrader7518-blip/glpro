GLPro Focus Areas

Four areas of everyday metabolic wellness that GLPro is designed to support. Choose one to explore in depth.

Healthy Glucose Levels

Your blood sugar rises after every meal and falls as insulin helps your cells take in glucose. Supporting that normal rhythm is the main focus of GLPro.

Explore focus area →

Sugar Cravings

Cravings often follow a sharp rise and fall in blood sugar. GLPro includes gymnema, an herb whose Hindi name, gurmar, translates loosely as “sugar destroyer”.

Explore focus area →

Steady Daily Energy

The 3 p.m. slump is often linked to what and when you ate. GLPro pairs blood sugar botanicals with alpha lipoic acid, a cofactor in cellular energy production.

Explore focus area →

Healthy Weight Goals

Weight and blood sugar are closely connected. GLPro is designed to complement a healthy eating and activity plan, not replace one.

One Formula, Four Focus Areas
