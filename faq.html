GLPro Frequently Asked Questions

Straight answers about GLPro's ingredients, directions, pricing, shipping and the money-back guarantee.

What is GLPro?
GLPro is a capsule dietary supplement made to support healthy blood sugar levels that are already within the normal range. Its label calls it a “Natural GLP-1 Booster”, and its formula combines botanicals such as cinnamon bark, gymnema sylvestre, banaba leaf and bitter melon with alpha lipoic acid and chromium picolinate. Read the full overview.
What ingredients are in GLPro?
Both GLPro sales pages list cinnamon bark, gymnema sylvestre, banaba leaf, bitter melon, alpha lipoic acid, chromium picolinate and juniper berry. One page also lists Berberine HCl, Yarrow Flower, White Mulberry Leaf, Vanadium, Magnesium Citrate, Zinc Glycinate and Taurine; the other also lists Guggul Resin, Licorice Root Extract and Cayenne Pepper. Individual amounts are not published, so check the Supplement Facts panel on your bottle for the exact formula.
How does GLPro work?
GLPro is designed to support the body's normal glucose metabolism with ingredients that have been studied for their role in insulin activity, carbohydrate handling and sweet cravings. Research on individual ingredients doesn't prove how the finished formula works for any one person. See how it works.
How do I take GLPro?
Take GLPro as directed on the bottle label. GLPro's sales page suggests taking it with a meal and a full glass of water, and each bottle (30 capsules) is sold as a 30-day supply. Don't exceed the label directions.
How long does one bottle last?
Each bottle holds 30 capsules and is sold as a 30-day supply. The 2-bottle package covers 60 days, the 3-bottle package 90 days and the 6-bottle package 180 days.
How long before I notice anything?
Experiences vary from person to person. The seller recommends the 3- or 6-bottle packages so you can use GLPro consistently over a longer period alongside healthy eating and activity. No specific result or timeline is guaranteed.

Yes. The seller offers a 60-day, 100% money-back guarantee. You can request a refund within 60 days of purchase, and the seller says refunds are issued within 48 hours of the request.
Does GLPro ship within the USA?
Yes. The 6-bottle package includes free US shipping. For the 2- and 3-bottle packages, shipping is calculated at checkout. A GLPro sales page states that orders are processed within 24–48 hours and that US delivery usually takes 3–5 business days.
Are there any side effects?
The seller describes GLPro as made from natural, non-GMO ingredients and reports no serious side effects. Several of its botanicals can lower blood sugar, so if you have diabetes, take insulin or glucose-lowering medicine, are pregnant or nursing, or have a medical condition, talk to your doctor before using it. Stop using it and seek advice if you notice anything unusual.
Is GLPro suitable for daily use?
Yes, it's designed to be taken every day as directed on the label, as part of a routine that includes balanced meals, regular movement and good sleep. It does not replace medication or medical care.
What makes GLPro different?
GLPro combines several well-known blood sugar support botanicals, a trace mineral and an antioxidant in one daily capsule, so you don't need a shelf full of separate bottles. According to its sales pages, it's made in the USA in a GMP-certified facility.
