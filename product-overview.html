What is it

What Is GLPro? Product Overview

Everything you need to know about GLPro: what it is, what's in it, how it's packaged and who it's made for.

A Daily Capsule for Healthy Blood Sugar

GLPro is a dietary supplement sold in capsule form. Its label reads “Support Blood Sugar — Natural GLP-1 Booster”, and it's made for adults who want to support glucose levels that are already within the normal range as part of a healthy lifestyle.

The idea is straightforward: rather than buying several single-ingredient products, you take one formula that brings together botanicals traditionally associated with blood sugar balance, a trace mineral involved in insulin action, and an antioxidant that plays a role in energy metabolism.

GLPro is not a medicine and isn't meant to diagnose, treat or prevent any disease. If you're managing diabetes, it isn't a substitute for your prescribed care.

Ingredients

What's Inside GLPro

These seven ingredients are named on both GLPro sales pages we reviewed.

Cinnamon Bark

Cinnamomum species

A kitchen spice that has been studied for its relationship with glucose metabolism. GLPro's sales pages list cinnamon bark as part of the blend.

Gymnema Sylvestre

Gymnema sylvestre leaf

A climbing plant from Ayurvedic tradition, long associated with sweet taste. Sales pages position it around sugar cravings.

Banaba Leaf

Lagerstroemia speciosa

A Southeast Asian leaf that naturally contains corosolic acid, a compound researched in relation to glucose handling.

Bitter Melon

Momordica charantia

A bitter, ridged gourd eaten across Asia, Africa and the Caribbean and used in traditional blood sugar remedies.

Alpha Lipoic Acid

ALA

An antioxidant compound the body makes in small amounts and that is also found in foods; it plays a role in energy metabolism.

Chromium Picolinate

Trace mineral

A form of the trace mineral chromium, which is involved in how insulin works in the body.

Juniper Berry

Juniperus communis

Small blue-purple berries rich in plant compounds, included in the formula as an antioxidant botanical.

About the full formula: the two GLPro sales pages list slightly different complete ingredient lists. One also names Berberine HCl, Yarrow Flower, White Mulberry Leaf, Vanadium, Magnesium Citrate, Zinc Glycinate and Taurine. The other also names Guggul Resin, Licorice Root Extract and Cayenne Pepper. Neither publishes individual amounts. Always read the Supplement Facts panel on the bottle you receive.
